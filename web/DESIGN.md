# Owo web: design direction

References pulled live (Oct 24): grey.co (Aeonik, -2px tracked 84px/600 headings, 12px-radius buttons), wise.com (900-weight display, 52px amount input, pill buttons, deep green ink on light), lemfi.com (pill buttons, off-white #F5F5EE), chippercash.com (Graphik, tight -0.9px headings).

Shared principles used: the amount is the hero (huge tabular numerals), one column money flow, pill CTAs, warm off-white canvas with white cards, tight-tracked display type.

Owo's own identity: cowrie shell mark (money in Yorubaland before the naira), naira green #0B6B43 with ochre #E39A2D accents on cream #F7F3EA; Bricolage Grotesque (display, numerals) + Manrope (body). No gradients beyond a faint ochre glow, no glass, no emoji.
